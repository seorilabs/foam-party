# Foam Party — Art Style Guide

Casual car-wash mobile game. Bright, clean, friendly. Assets must sit on top of a
mint/teal car-wash scene and a warm-orange procedurally drawn car.

## Style family
Flat vector illustration with **thick uniform outlines** and soft cel shading.
Chubby, rounded, cute silhouettes — no realism, no gradients-as-rendering, no
photographic texture. One flat base tone per shape plus a single darker shade
band and a single soft highlight. Reads clearly at small sizes (down to 64px).

## Outline
Dark navy outline `#123246`, consistent weight, fully closed around every shape.
Slightly rounded corners on the outline. No black; navy only.

## Shading
- Base fill: one flat color.
- Shade: same hue ~12% darker, on the lower/right third only.
- Highlight: one soft off-white `#fff7dd` blob upper-left, low opacity.
- No ground shadow (assets are isolated).

## Palette (hex)
- Aqua BG family: `#87e3e9`, `#9ff0ef`, `#6dd0d1` (scene context, don't fill assets with it)
- Water/blue: `#49a7ff`, `#b7f0ff`
- Foam/soap white: `#f7fbff`, `#d9f8ff`
- Coin/gold: `#ffce3d`, `#e0a818`, `#9a7400`
- Success green: `#39d98a`, `#1f8a55`
- Warm accent: `#ff9f5a`, `#ff6b6b`
- Purple accent: `#a855f7`, `#7a35c8`
- Outline/ink: `#123246`, `#0d3b55`

## Proportions & light
Chunky forms, generous rounding, thick outline. Light source upper-left.
Single soft highlight, single shade band. Playful, energetic, squeaky-clean mood.

## Framing (required for every asset)
Centered single object, isolated on a pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background, no ground
shadow, no text, no lettering, filling ~80% of the frame with even margins.
